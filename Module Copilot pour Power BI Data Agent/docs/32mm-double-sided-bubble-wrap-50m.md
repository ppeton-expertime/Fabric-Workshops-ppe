# 32 mm Double Sided Bubble Wrap 50m

## Product Overview
The 32 mm Double Sided Bubble Wrap 50m is a protective packaging roll with cushioning bubbles designed to absorb impacts during handling and transport. The double-sided construction provides balanced protection around wrapped items.

## Key Features
- Bubble size: 32 mm
- Roll length: 50 m
- Double-sided protective structure
- Flexible material for wrapping irregular shapes

## Typical Use Cases
- Packaging fragile products
- Surface protection in storage or relocation
- Layering inside cartons for shock reduction

## Basic Usage Instructions
1. Measure the required wrap length.
2. Cut from the roll with suitable cutting tools.
3. Wrap the item with bubbles facing the product when required by your process.
4. Secure with tape or stretch film.

## Storage and Handling
- Store in a cool, dry area.
- Keep away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
- Avoid heavy loads on top of the roll.

## Safety Notes
- Keep plastic packaging materials away from young children.
- Do not use near open flames.
