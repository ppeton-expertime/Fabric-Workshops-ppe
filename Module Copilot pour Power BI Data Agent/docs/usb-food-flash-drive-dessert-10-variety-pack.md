The USB Food Flash Drive - Dessert 10 Drive Variety Pack includes ten novelty USB drives styled like dessert items. It combines playful design with practical data storage for personal, educational, or promotional use.

## Key Features
- Set of 10 themed USB drives
- Dessert-inspired external designs
- Portable and reusable storage media
- Suitable for giveaways and team kits

## Typical Use Cases
- Sharing training documents and media
- Event gifts or marketing bundles
- Personal backup of small to medium files

## Basic Usage Instructions
1. Insert a drive into a compatible USB port.
2. Wait for automatic recognition by the operating system.
3. Copy files to or from the drive.
4. Safely eject before removing.

## Compatibility
- Windows, macOS, and Linux (USB mass storage support)
- No additional driver required in most modern systems

## Care and Handling
- Avoid bending or forcing the connector.
- Keep away from liquids and extreme heat.
- Store each drive in a clean, dry compartment.
