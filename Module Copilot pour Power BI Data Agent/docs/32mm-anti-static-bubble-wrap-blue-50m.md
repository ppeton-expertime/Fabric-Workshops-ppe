# 32 mm Anti Static Bubble Wrap (Blue) 50m

## Product Overview
The 32 mm Anti Static Bubble Wrap (Blue) 50m is designed to protect sensitive electronic items from both physical shock and electrostatic discharge (ESD) exposure during handling and shipment.

## Key Features
- Bubble size: 32 mm
- Roll length: 50 m
- Anti-static formulation for ESD-sensitive components
- Blue color for quick anti-static identification

## Typical Use Cases
- Shipping PC components and peripherals
- Protecting circuit boards and electronic modules
- ESD-aware storage and internal transfers

## Basic Usage Instructions
1. Cut the required wrap length from the roll.
2. Wrap electronic items fully, covering edges and corners.
3. Seal with anti-static tape if your process requires ESD compliance.
4. Place in an appropriate carton or ESD-safe container.

## Storage and Compliance Notes
- Store away from moisture and direct heat.
- Replace stock according to your ESD handling policy.
- Verify compatibility with your internal ESD standards.

## Safety Notes
- Plastic film can create suffocation hazards; keep away from children.
- Keep away from ignition sources.
