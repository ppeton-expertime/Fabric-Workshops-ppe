# Void Fill 400 L Bag (White) 400L

## Product Overview
The Void Fill 400 L Bag (White) 400L is a high-capacity filler material bag used to stabilize products inside shipping cartons by occupying empty space and reducing movement in transit.

## Key Features
- Capacity: 400 liters
- White bag format for clean visual handling
- Fast deployment for carton stabilization
- Suitable for mixed-size shipment operations

## Typical Use Cases
- Filling gaps in large shipping cartons
- Improving load stability for multi-item orders
- Supporting lightweight protective packaging strategies

## Basic Usage Instructions
1. Open the bag at the designated access point.
2. Dispense required filler quantity into the carton.
3. Place filler around products to minimize internal movement.
4. Seal carton and verify item stability.

## Storage and Handling
- Store in a dry location.
- Reseal or close bag opening after use when possible.
- Keep away from sharp objects that may puncture the bag.

## Safety Notes
- Keep plastic material away from children.
- Do not place near flames or excessive heat.
