# Ride on Big Wheel Monster Truck (Black) 1/12 Scale

The Ride on Big Wheel Monster Truck (Black) 1/12 scale is a toy vehicle designed for role-play and light indoor or outdoor play on smooth surfaces. It features a monster-truck style with oversized wheels and a durable body shape.

## Key Features
- 1/12 scale toy format
- Big-wheel monster-truck styling
- Black body finish
- Low-profile seat area for stable riding posture

## Typical Use Cases
- Pretend play sessions
- Kids activity areas
- Gift item for vehicle-themed play

## Basic Usage Instructions
1. Place the toy on a flat and obstacle-free surface.
2. Ensure all visible parts are correctly assembled.
3. Supervise young children during use.
4. Clean wheels and body after outdoor play.

## Safety Notes
- Recommended for age groups indicated by the manufacturer.
- Always use under adult supervision.
- Do not use on steep slopes, near roads, or near stairs.
- Check for loose parts before each use.

## Care and Storage
- Store in a dry location away from direct sunlight.
- Wipe with a soft damp cloth.
- Avoid strong solvents or abrasive cleaners.
